Things worth saying out loud

  • Why not just use Switch()? — Material 3's built-in switch hardcodes its animation. To change the feel you have to own the component. Say this at 2:00 so nobody comments it.
  • dampingRatio = how much it wobbles. 1f = no overshoot, 0.5f = medium bouncy, 0.2f = very bouncy. Compose ships these as Spring.DampingRatioMediumBouncy etc.
  • stiffness = how fast it gets there. 200 low, 400 medium-low, 1500 medium (the default). Higher = snappier.
  • Springs ignore duration — that's the point. Interrupt one mid-flight and it keeps its velocity instead of restarting. Demo this by spam-clicking the toggle.
  • Real M3 sizes: track 52×32dp, thumb 16dp off / 24dp on / 28dp pressed. Thumb centre travels 16dp → 36dp.
